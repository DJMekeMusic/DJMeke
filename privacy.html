Privacy Policy

DJ Meke Analytics

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how DJ Meke Analytics accesses, uses, and stores information when connecting to authorized social media accounts and services.

2. Purpose of the Service

DJ Meke Analytics is a private analytics tool used to monitor, organize, and analyze the performance of DJ Meke's own social media accounts and digital content.

3. Information We Access

When an authorized account is connected, DJ Meke Analytics may access account profile information, account statistics, video information, and video performance metrics made available through the relevant platform's authorized APIs.

Depending on the permissions granted by the account owner, this information may include profile information, follower statistics, video identifiers, video titles or descriptions, view counts, likes, comments, shares, and other content performance information.

4. TikTok Data

When DJ Meke Analytics connects to TikTok, TikTok information is accessed only through authorized TikTok APIs and according to the permissions granted by the TikTok account owner.

5. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by DJ Meke Analytics is used for internal analytics, performance tracking, reporting, historical comparison, and optimization of DJ Meke's digital content.

6. Data Sharing

DJ Meke Analytics does not sell personal information or social media account data. Information accessed through authorized integrations is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

7. Data Storage and Security

Analytics information may be stored for the purpose of maintaining historical performance records. Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information and access credentials.

8. Access and Authorization

Access to connected social media accounts is based on authorization provided by the account owner. Authorization may be revoked through the applicable social media platform when supported by that platform.

9. Third-Party Platforms

DJ Meke Analytics may integrate with third-party services such as TikTok. The collection and processing of information by those platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to DJ Meke Analytics, its integrations, or applicable requirements.

11. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official DJ Meke website.
